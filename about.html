About Us

Who are we?

A space designed for

growth

Prestige Innovation Hub (PiHub) is a technology-focused environment created for people who want to learn, work, create and grow. We provide a comfortable and productive space where individuals, professionals, students and teams can focus on their work while gaining access to practical technology education and opportunities for collaboration.

Whether you need a quiet place to work, want to develop a new digital skill or need a professional space to collaborate, PiHub brings the environment and resources together to help you move forward.

What we do?

We provide the space, skills, and support you need to turn ideas into progress

Practical technology training designed to help individuals develop valuable digital skills.

Comfortable and reliable workspaces for individuals, remote workers, freelancers and professionals.

An environment that encourages collaboration,networking, knowledge sharing and Innovation.
